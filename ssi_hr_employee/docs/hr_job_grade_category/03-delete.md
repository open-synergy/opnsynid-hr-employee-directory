# Delete Job Grade Category

> **Module:** `ssi_hr_employee`\
> **Model:** `hr.job_grade_category`\
> **Menu:** Human Resource > Configuration > Organization > Job Grade Categories\
> **Actor:** user in group _Job Grade Category_\
> **Requires:** `01-create`

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** The job grade category exists and is not used by other records.
- **Access:** User is in group _Job Grade Category_.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Configuration > Organization > Job Grade Categories**
   menu.
2. Find and open the record to delete.
3. Click the **Action** menu, then click **Delete**.
4. Click **OK** on the confirmation dialog.

## Post-Condition

- The record is removed from the list of Job Grade Categories.
